Season-4 Rules & Regulations

  • 1️⃣ Match Format: Each match will be played with 8 overs per side.
  • 2️⃣ Ball Rules: A new ball will be used for each innings. If the ball is lost, damaged, or goes out of ground, a used ball will be used based on umpire/organiser decision.
  • 3️⃣ Match Delay: If matches are delayed due to time or other reasons, overs may be reduced (2–6 overs per side). Final decision will be taken by the organiser.
  • 4️⃣ Player Participation: All registered players must play in the tournament. Teams should rotate players in matches.
  • 5️⃣ Tie Matches: In Eliminator, Semi-Final, or Final — if match is tied, a Super Over (1 over) will decide the winner.
  • 6️⃣ Umpire Decision: Final and binding. No arguments will be accepted.
  • 7️⃣ No Extras: No LBW and No Leg Byes in the tournament.
  • 8️⃣ Player Availability: No replacements allowed. If a player is absent, the team must play with available players only.
  • 9️⃣ Bowling Rules: No throwing or illegal bowling action. If warned by umpire, bowler may be banned from bowling for entire tournament.
  • 🔟 Runner Rules: Only 1 runner per team allowed under conditions. No runner allowed in last over. Runner must be from playing 11.
  • 1️⃣1️⃣ League Match Interruption: If match is cancelled due to rain/light, 1 point each will be awarded.
  • 1️⃣2️⃣ Knockout Interruption: If Eliminator/Semi/Final cannot be played, table topper will be winner.
  • 1️⃣3️⃣ Final Match Issue: If final is interrupted and cannot continue, table topper wins.
  • 1️⃣4️⃣ Communication: Only Captain can speak with umpire. Others interfering may lead to penalties (fielders/overs reduction).
  • 1️⃣5️⃣ Performance Tracking: Scores, MOM, MOS, Best Player etc. will be recorded using CricHeroes App.
  • 1️⃣6️⃣ Team Lock: After auction, no player swap/change allowed.
  • 1️⃣7️⃣ Discipline: No fighting, abuse, or arguments. Violators will be removed from tournament.
  • 1️⃣8️⃣ Responsibility: Organisers are not responsible for personal belongings or unexpected incidents.
  • 1️⃣9️⃣ Misconduct: Any intentional conflict or targeting will lead to immediate suspension.
  • 2️⃣0️⃣ Spirit: We encourage sportsmanship, enjoyment, and unity throughout the tournament.
